Churn Rate by Geography

Germany has the highest churn (32.4%), about 16.3 percentage points above France.

Churn by Age Group

Churn peaks in ages 50–59 (56.0%) and is lowest for Under 30 (7.6%).

Active Member Status vs Churn

Inactive members churn at 26.9% versus 14.3% for active members.

Credit Card Type Breakdown

DIAMOND has the highest card-type churn (21.8%); GOLD is lowest (19.3%).

How to read this: Churn rate = customers with Exited = 1 ÷ unique customers in each segment. Each chart uses the same 10,000-record dataset. Scope: identifiers and fields not used in these four views are excluded from the dashboard; this is descriptive, not causal. Minimum sample size: segments with fewer than 100 customers are excluded from the charts (none of the displayed segments fall below this threshold). Rates can vary with segment size; compare both rate and customer count.
